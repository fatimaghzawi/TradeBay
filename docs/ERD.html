TradeBay ERD — professional v1.1 · 52 collections · User → Membership → Role → Permission

Money-correct B2B marketplace. A person is users; they act for a company through business_memberships. Role lives on the membership, never on the user. Invitation assigns a Role, not raw permissions. Selling still requires a verified supplier profile. Procurement is RFQ → quote → purchase order (line items are collections, not embeds). Customer finance is invoice → payment → financial_transactions (what the buyer owes). Platform money is held → fee → payable → payout with its own platform_transactions ledger (where the money went). Settings are platform / VAT / business letterhead. Snapshot rates onto invoices; secrets stay in env. Messaging is general-purpose: any two companies can hold a thread on an RFQ, quotation, order, dispute, product, or nothing at all — negotiation is one context, never the only one. A message is never a commercial record; it only references the authoritative quotation / order. PK · UK · FK · FK? · EMB nested · DN snapshot. Solid = required. Dashed = optional.
